# NBA simulator vs real DraftKings lines (2026-11-07)

132 finished 2026-27 games, Oct 20 – Nov 6, 10,933 player props (points, rebounds, assists
and combos), DraftKings lines from ESPN's public odds feed. Walk-forward ratings, 2,000 sims
per game, real players and starters. Intervals resample games. In-sample: the unders pattern
below was found on these same games, so treat it as a lead, not proof.

**The sim's percentages run high.** Share of picks that actually hit:

| Sim says | Hit | DK break-even | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55–60% | 52.7% | 53.7% | −1.9% |
| 60–65% | 54.9% | 54.1% | +1.8% |
| 65–70% | 57.5% | 54.3% | +6.0% |
| 70–80% | 57.8% | 54.9% | +5.5% |
| 80%+ | 55.2% | 54.6% | +1.2% |

**DraftKings' lines beat the sim as forecasts:** player points error 4.91 vs 5.03, margin 10.92
vs 11.77, totals 14.17 vs 15.57. The sim's totals run 5.6 points high.

**Spreads: no edge.** Sim 55%+ spread picks hit about 48% (40 of 84); break-even is about 52%.

**Overs: no edge.** Sim 60%+ overs hit 54.4%, ROI −0.4% (second half −4.3%). Blind overs: −10.5%.

**Unders (points, assists, PA, PR, PRA, RA) with sim 60%+:** 58.3% hit [55.2, 61.6], ROI +8.1%
[+2.1, +14.3], n=2,933. First half 55.0%, second half 61.5%. One prop per player: 58.5%, ROI
+6.0% [−0.5, +12.3]. Blind unders: 52.5%, ROI −2.1%. Sim 80%+ unders: 52.1%, ROI −3.5%.
